Eastern Europe

Russia
The Russian tricolor was first raised in the 17th century on the "Oryol", Russia's first naval warship; however, the exact arrangement of its white, blue, and red stripes on that flag remains a historical mystery to this day.
Bulgaria
Bulgaria's white, green, and red flag was created based on the Russian tricolor as a gesture of gratitude for the country's liberation during the Russo-Turkish War; the blue stripe was replaced with a green one, symbolizing the nation's agriculture and freedom.
Czech Republic
The flag of the Czech Republic is identical to the flag of the former Czechoslovakia.
Romania
The blue, yellow, and red colors of the Romanian flag symbolize the three historical provinces that united to form a single state: blue represents Wallachia, yellow represents Transylvania, and red represents Moldavia.
